# LifeOS 1.5.1

Installed on 29 October 2026 at `C:\Users\USER\AppData\Local\Programs\LifeOS`.

The visual update adds local CSS glass materials, blur, a floating sidebar and toolbar, translucent cards, soft background lighting, and light/dark layouts. The same user can access all tools; there is no administrator role.

Personal essentials now includes central reminders, manual renewal-date changes, visible notes and repayment history, and correction of mistaken repayments. Small business includes correction of project records. All six new tables are available in CSV exports and the printable report. Net worth explains account balances, active asset values and individual planned loans.
